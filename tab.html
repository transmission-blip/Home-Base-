Tab Transcriber

Tuner Recorder
–
 

Play one note at a time and the tab writes itself. New takes are added after what's already there.

Tap any spot on a string and type a fret (two digits quickly for 10 and up). Arrow keys move, Backspace clears. Each column is a sixteenth note. Listening hears one note at a time: for chords, or notes left ringing over each other, type them in by hand.
