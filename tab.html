Tab Transcriber

Tuner Recorder
–
 

Play a take, press Stop, and it's written out as tab. New takes are added after what's already there.

Tap any spot on a string and type a fret (two digits quickly for 10 and up). Arrow keys move, Backspace clears. Each column is a sixteenth note. Roman numerals above the tab mark where the fretting hand shifts: V means first finger at the 5th fret. The same note can be played in several places on the neck, so strings and positions are a best guess at the easiest fingering, not something the microphone can hear. Chord detection uses Basic Pitch and runs entirely in your browser.
